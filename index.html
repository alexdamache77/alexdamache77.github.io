About me

I'm a Senior Product Designer based in Copenhagen. Since 2019 I've been at EcoTree International, where I run design solo — UX, UI, and the design system.

I care about research. I've learned that the best interfaces come from really understanding the problem first — talking to users, digging into data, then sketching. Figma comes later.

I also use AI heavily in my workflow, both for research and for building tools that make the design process faster.
